When preparing the board for the next turn, do the following:
*  Any exhausted officers and military units in the Army of North America become active.
   Any active local alliances are exhausted.
*  Move any remaining Family members on the Smuggler's Path to the rightmost slot. Then, in turn order from the Chairman, Smugglers in America may purchase another Tea Crate and spot on the Smuggler's Path to place their Family member there.
