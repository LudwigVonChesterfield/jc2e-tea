For the duration of the scenario, the firms operate in the American mainland, not India. The *India Treasury* space holds revenue gained from trade in America (instead of India). This treasury is used to pay dividends. Any remainder is then transferred to the London treasury.

:::note
The law to deregulate **is not present** in this scenario. However, the rules regarding deregulation are present from the beginning, with the following modifications.
:::

## The Firms Phase

After the Family phase is complete and before the Hiring phase, players will now perform the Firms phase. The Firms phase has two steps: Firm Investments and Firm Strategy.

## Firm Investment

A firm manager may close a bank loan.
*  Pay £5 from the firm's treasury.
*  Remove a red cube in the firm's Shares box.
*  Raise the {frontmatter._["credit.track"]} by 1 space.

## Firm Strategy

All firms in this scenario will trade in America, not India. When choosing a strategy, a firm is not deciding what sea zone they will be placed in, but which port they are going to initiate their trade at.

<div class="indent">
### Tea Auction

During the tea auction, a firm may acquire {frontmatter._["tea.token.plural"]}, using the
funds in their Trade Bid space. Therefore, a firm manager should place any funds needed for
both the tea auction and trade when deciding on their strategy.

### Firms and Storms

When rolling for storms during the Events in America phase, treat all firm ships as
if they were in the area indicated by the firm's strategy. However, if the firm's chosen
strategy was “Hobnob,” that firm's ships will not be affected by storms.
</div>

## Filling Orders as a Firm

At the start of the Tea phase,
all firms reveal their strategy and **declare any regions they wish to trade Tea in**. Make a check with dice equal to:
*  The amount of cash allocated to the strategy.
*  Minus the amount of unrest cubes in the declared regions_.

The cash is then paid to the bank .
If at least one success is rolled, the firm may trade. Each firm that
successfully trades has an initiative value equal to the total of their ships minus the number of
successful dice (1s and 2s) they rolled. This number can be negative.

The current smuggler card determines smuggler initiative.

Players then fill orders starting with the smugglers, firm or Company with the lowest
initiative and proceeding to the highest. When it is their turn, the player trading
fills as many orders as possible. Any ties in initiative are decided by the
{frontmatter._["role.govt.governor"]}.

Firms fill a number of open orders up to the number of ships they own **plus one**.
Orders must be filled starting with the associated port listed on their strategy. When
filling an order, you must be able to trace a path back to this port through any
number of orders that you have filled.

   *  **Previously-Filled Orders.**
      Players may fill orders previously filled this turn by other players. The filling
      of a previously-filled order will generate half of its listed value, rounding down.
      _This matches the other Deregulation scenarios_.

   *  **Trading with Tea.**
      As a firm with {frontmatter._["tea.token.plural"]} remaining is filling orders, it may
      place one {frontmatter._["tea.token.singular"]} at an order within its declared regions.
      Placing a {frontmatter._["tea.token.singular"]} will generate an additional order's listed value.
      Tea may be traded at an order that is already filled.
      _You may not place {frontmatter._["tea.token.singular"]} on orders that already have one._

Finally, advance your firm's Value marker one space for each £6 you added to the firm's
India treasury this turn. Any remaining {frontmatter._["tea.token.plural"]} in the Trade Bid
space should be returned to the supply.

## Firm Expenses and Dividends

A firm pays expenses and dividends, with the following additions.

<div class="indent">
### Firm Expenses

Each firm must pay for the following expenses:
* Pay £1 for each ship on the firm's charter.
* Pay £1 for each share **and bank loan** in the firm's Share box or pay £ equal to the firm value,
whichever is higher.

### Emergency Loans

A firm facing dissolution may acquire a single emergency loan. Treat this the same as an Emergency Investment, using a red debt cube instead of a family marker. Lower the {frontmatter._["credit.track"]} by 1. _If a firm is at the ten shares limit, a debt cube is still added to the Shares box._

### Firm Dividends

When paying dividends, pay the Bank £1 per red cube in the Shares box. The amount paid to the bank **does not count** towards the firm's special retirement amount.
</div>

### Merging

When Firms merge, transfer Tea Crates and red debt cubes belonging to both Firms to the resulting Firm.

### Bankruptcy

In addition to everything else, Firm's Manager places an additional family member on the Debtor's Prison.
