In _{frontmatter._["game.title"]}_, Parliament has a bit more responsibility
and reach. The following are additions to this phase:

## The {frontmatter._["deck.laws.plural"]}

While the Prime Minister is selecting a law to put before Parliament, the
{frontmatter._["role.govt.tariffs.full"]} is responsible for choosing a
recommendation from the Board of Trade. They do this by drawing and revealing
two Act cards from the {frontmatter._["deck.laws.singular"]} Deck. The
{frontmatter._["role.govt.tariffs"]} will choose one Act to be enacted
if the vote passes. The other act will be enacted if the vote fails.

After the Prime Minister reads the law and marks its starting support
(printed on bottom right of law), the {frontmatter._["role.govt.tariffs"]}
reads aloud the chosen Trade Act to be enacted. The Prime Minister and
{frontmatter._["role.govt.tariffs"]} should allow some time for debate
before proceeding to voting.

During the vote, the {frontmatter._["role.govt.tariffs"]} is not restricted
in voting either way.

*  __Vacant {frontmatter._["role.govt.tariffs"]}.__
   If the {frontmatter._["role.govt.tariffs"]} is vacant, before drawing
   any law cards, the Prime Minister must appoint a _{frontmatter._["role.govt.tariffs2"]}_
   for the turn. The Prime Minister is free to appoint any player, other
   than themselves and the player who was the {frontmatter._["role.govt.tariffs.full"]}
   most recently. Hand the appointed player the {frontmatter._["deck.laws.singular"]} Deck.
   The {frontmatter._["role.govt.tariffs2"]} performs all of the same actions the
   {frontmatter._["role.govt.tariffs"]} would during Parliament Meets.

## Vote Sources

Additionally to other sources of votes, pensioners with a Tea Crate on them have a vote icon. You may rotate or flip the pensioner to indicate they have voted.

## Resolving the Vote: Additions

Based on how the vote resolves, one of the Trade Acts will be enacted.
*  Apply any immediate effects of the enacted Trade Act and place it next to the Scenario Board.
   If the newly enacted Trade Act is a _Colonial Duty_, place it on top of the previous _Colonial Duty_.
*  If the vote failed, but the Secretary of Colonial Department did not vote against it, they get a Passed Law token.

Discard the other Trade Act that was not enacted.

## The Vote to Regulate Tea

In this scenario, the Prime Minister may initiate a special session of Parliament at the start of the turn before the London Season phase. To do this, the {frontmatter._["credit.track"]} must be less than either the Company Debt or the amount of stored {frontmatter._["tea.token.plural"]}. If the {frontmatter._["credit.track"]} is less than both the Company Debt and the amount of {frontmatter._["tea.token.plural"]} present on the map, the Prime Minister **must** initiate a special session to vote on Tea Regulation.

Secretary issues Board of Trade proposals during this.

When voting, the starting support should start with the amount of stored {frontmatter._["tea.token.plural"]} minus the amount of {frontmatter._["credit.track"]}.

:::note[Example of the starting support]
If the {frontmatter._["credit.track"]} is 5 and the Company has 8 stored {frontmatter._["tea.token.plural"]},
`8 - 5 = 3` and support for the vote would start at +3. Similarly, if the {frontmatter._["credit.track"]} is 7 and the Company has 6 stored {frontmatter._["tea.token.plural"]}, `6 - 7 = -1`, and support for the vote would start at -1.
:::

If the vote passes, increase the {frontmatter._["tea.track"]} by one space. Place the Standing marker on the “S” space on the track and place 3 Company ships in the Atlantic. The Prime Minister gains a passed law token. Change the {frontmatter._["role.company.tea"]} to the {frontmatter._["role.company.tea2"]}, per the rules in the {frontmatter._["role.company.tea2"]} section. Refer to the Tea Regulation card for additional changes.

Additionally, clear half of the debt from Colonial Department.

If the law fails, increase the value of Company shares by placing (or flipping) the VP overlay in the Court. Shares cannot be worth more than +/- 3 victory points. __Unlike other proposed laws, this law is not associated with a policy or an act from the {frontmatter._["deck.laws.singular"]} and the Prime Minister does not change hands if it fails nor will this law be discarded. The Prime Minister may also vote against the law.__

## The Vote for a General Indemnity

If Tea Regulation is passed,

In this scenario, the Prime Minister may initiate a special session of Parliament at the start of the turn before the London Season phase.

Secretary issues Board of Trade proposals during this.

When voting, the starting support should be equal to the negative of the amount of pieces in Debtor's Prison and Smuggler's Jail.

If the vote passes, the Prime Minister gets a Passed Law token, then return all pieces from the Debtor's Prison and Smuggler's jail, and flip the Smuggler's jail card. Additionally, lower the Value of Tea by 1.

Additionally, clear half of the debt from the Company and Firms (rounding down).

If the law fails, the opposition leader becomes the Prime Minister, and additionally increase the Value of Tea by 1. The pardon may be proposed again.
