## New Company Shares

For each space the Company Debt is lowered (as shares are added), raise the {frontmatter._["credit.track"]} by 1.

## Smuggling

// Players may opt not to place the Opportunity Marker on any action, and instead place a Family member on the Smuggler's Path card.

When Seeking a Share, players may place a Family member on the Smuggler's Path instead of the Company Share track.

To do so, they must choose a spot with a cost they can pay on the card plus pay the Value of Tea rounded down.

Rounding down, for each Value of Tea - the last spot that was not taken by a player gets a Tea Crate at the end of Family Phase. If there's already a Tea Crate on the spot, and the next spot has less Tea Crates, it gets the Tea Crate instead. When filling an order, a Smuggler with Tea Crates on its spot additionally gets half the value of the order for every Tea Crate on it.
